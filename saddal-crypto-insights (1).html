Event-risk gate

If any box is ticked, every trade signal becomes NO TRADE. Check these first: Forex Factory calendar · Investing.com calendar · TradingEconomics · Token unlocks · BTC ETF flows · Liquidations · Crypto headlines. Links may change over time; search the site name if one fails.

Market context

Fear & Greed, funding and long/short positioning load when you analyze a pair.

Market scanner

Signal strength is a 0–100% score of the technical setup: multi-timeframe alignment 28%, indicator confirmation 28%, momentum 20%, volume and liquidity 14%, market conditions 10%. It is not a probability of profit or a win rate. Scanning uses price and volume only; funding and news context are not scored, and the event-risk gate still applies. Each pair costs two data requests; if Binance returns a rate-limit error the scan stops and says so.

Trade history

Open a trade with “Paper trade this signal” in the signal panel or the Paper button in the scanner. Positions are checked against 60-second price snapshots while this page is open (stop or TP-1 hit), net of your round-trip cost, so a level touched between snapshots can be missed. Margin uses your leverage setting. A few paper trades prove nothing: wait for dozens before judging a strategy.
